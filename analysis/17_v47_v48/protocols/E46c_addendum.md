# E46c addendum to the E46 protocol: near-identical samples inside the I-SPY2 pembrolizumab comparison

Written 2026-11-05 after an independent check of E46b noticed that, in the positive control of E46b, a sample matched another sample of ISPY2_pembro at 0.86, and that the top pair includes the two samples to which all flags of E46 part D pointed. Post hoc; no expectations.

1. Reads no outcome: gene-centered Pearson correlations (as E46b) among all samples of ISPY2_pembro. List every pair above 0.8 with arm and processing label (ADJ_P of E32_trials), and each sample's correlation with the comparison's mean profile.
2. If pairs exist: remove one sample of each pair (the second in sample order) and repeat the six-comparison pools of CD8A, the three-gene mean and the T-cell-inflamed list, as a sensitivity analysis. The sample count of the comparison in the main text does not change unless these are shown to be the same patient; expression alone cannot show that.

Outputs: results/E46c_pembro_pairs.csv, results/E46c_sensitivity.csv.
